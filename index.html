1.1.2 Pressing triggers

The modern game relies on intelligent pressing, quick transitions, and positional discipline to dominate possession and control the tempo.

The goalkeeper is the first attacker, starting plays with precise distribution under pressure. Centre backs must read the game, step into midfield, and launch counter‑attacks with line‑breaking passes.

Total distance covered: 12 km2 (pitch coverage), maximum speed 34.2 m2/s, VO₂ max 62 ml3/kg/min, power output 980 W4.

Optimal hydration: drink 250 ml H2O every 15 min, 12O6 for energy, lactate threshold at 4 mmol·Lblood⁻¹, and O2 uptake efficiency.

Old school: long ball to target man, man‑marking zonal, two sweepers, kick and rush — all evolved.

Key actions: pressing trap, half‑space occupation, transition moments, and second‑ball recovery.
